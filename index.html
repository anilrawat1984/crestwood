About Crestwood Play & Care

Growing young minds with care & joy — right in the heart of Palm Resort, Raj Nagar Extension.

Who We Are

Introduction

Crestwood Play & Care is a preschool and day care centre located at Center G1-003, Palm Resort, Raj Nagar Extension, Ghaziabad. We offer Playgroup, Nursery, LKG and UKG programs along with a full Day Care facility for working parents.

Our campus has thoughtfully designed spaces — a class corner for focused learning, an activity corner for creativity, a safe play area and a peaceful dormitory where day care children can rest. The entire premises is under CCTV surveillance, so parents can be assured of their child's safety.

We follow a play-based, activity-oriented approach where children learn by doing, exploring and having fun — building strong foundations in language, numbers, social skills and confidence.

Crestwood Vision

What we dream for every child

🌟 Our Vision

Our Mission

  • Provide a safe, hygienic and secure environment where children feel at home.
  • Make learning joyful through play, stories, music, art and hands-on activities.
  • Develop every child's social, emotional, physical and cognitive skills.
  • Prepare children to step confidently into formal schooling.
  • Partner closely with parents in their child's growth journey.
🌱

Our Approach

  • Learning through play – children learn best when they are having fun.
  • Child-centric – every child grows at their own pace and in their own way.
  • Activity based – hands-on experiences in our activity corner.
  • Values & manners – sharing, kindness, respect and good habits.
  • Care beyond class – a nurturing day care for working families.

Safety First

Your child's safety is our priority

We understand how important it is for parents to feel confident about where their child spends the day. That's why safety is built into everything we do at Crestwood.

  • Entire premises covered by CCTV security cameras
  • Secure, gated campus inside Palm Resort
  • Children handed over only to parents or authorised guardians
  • Child-safe furniture, rounded edges and covered sockets
  • First-aid kit and trained staff on campus
  • Constant adult supervision in class, play and rest areas

Which class is right for my child?

Age is calculated as on 31st March of the admission year.

Age criteria may be relaxed slightly at the discretion of the management. Please speak to us if your child's age falls between two classes.

Admission Process

5 simple steps to join Crestwood

📁

Documents Required

  • Duly filled admission form
  • Child's birth certificate (copy)
  • Child's Aadhaar card (copy, if available)
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs of the child
  • 2 passport-size photographs each of both parents / guardians
  • Aadhaar card / ID proof of parents (copy)
  • Address proof (copy)
  • Child's immunisation / vaccination record (copy)
  • Medical information or allergies, if any

Please carry the originals for verification at the time of admission.
